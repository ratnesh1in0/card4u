Frequently Asked Questions

Is card4u really 100% free with no hidden charges?

Yes! card4u is completely free. We do not have any pricing tiers, subscriptions, paywalls, or hidden fees. You can design, customize, and share unlimited interactive digital cards with zero watermarks.

Do my friends or guests need to download an app?

No! Every card is a fast, responsive web link that opens smoothly in any web browser on iPhone, Android, tablets, and computers.

How does the Scratch-to-Reveal card work?

When you create your card in Studio, you can type any secret note or surprise (like a gift code, flight announcement, or heartfelt confession). When the recipient receives the card, it is shielded by metallic gold foil that they scratch away interactively!

How do I share my card after creating it?

After customizing your card, click "Share Card" to copy a direct link, send it with 1-click on WhatsApp, or download a printable QR code!
